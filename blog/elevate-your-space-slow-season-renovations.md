## Why Slow Season Is the Ideal Time to Renovate

### 1. Contractors Are Available—and Focused on Your Project

During high season, skilled contractors and craftsmen are stretched thin across dozens of projects. Lead times stretch, timelines slip, and quality can suffer when teams are overbooked.

During the slow season (May through October), availability opens up. That means:

- **Faster project start dates** — no months-long waitlists
- **More dedicated attention** from your design and build team
- **Tighter timelines** because crews aren't pulled in every direction
- **Better communication** throughout the entire process

When you're our priority project, you get our best work.

---

### 2. No Disruption to Guests or Daily Life

If your property is a vacation rental or you're planning to list it, renovation during occupancy is not just inconvenient—it's costly in reputation and reviews.

The slow season gives you a clear runway:

- Work gets done without impacting guests or bookings
- You're not turning away high-season reservations during construction
- Your property is polished, photographed, and listed **before** peak demand returns

---

### 3. More Time Means Better Decisions

Rushing a renovation leads to costly mistakes—wrong materials, poor layouts, regrettable shortcuts.

The slow season gives you the gift of **time to plan properly**:

- Review designs without pressure
- Source premium materials thoughtfully
- Make changes before work begins (not during)
- Avoid the expensive change orders that come from deciding on the fly

---

### 4. Be Ready to Command Higher Rental Rates

A renovated kitchen or bathroom isn't just more beautiful—it's more profitable.

Properties with updated, high-quality finishes in Puerto Vallarta consistently:

- Attract higher-end guests willing to pay premium nightly rates
- Earn stronger reviews, driving more bookings
- Stand out in a competitive rental market

Renovate now, and your investment starts working for you from day one of high season.

## Your Pre-High Season Planning Checklist

Use the slow season to plan carefully. Here's where to focus your thinking:

### ✅ Define Your Goals and Budget

Start with clarity. Ask yourself:

- What's the primary purpose of this renovation—personal enjoyment, resale value, rental income?
- Which spaces have the highest impact: kitchen, bathrooms, living areas?
- What's a realistic budget, including a 10–15% contingency?

Having answers upfront keeps the project on track and on budget.

---

### ✅ Choose Materials Built for the Coastal Climate

Puerto Vallarta's humidity, salt air, and heat are hard on low-quality materials. Plan with durability in mind:

- **Parota wood** and other tropical hardwoods that resist warping and cracking
- **Marine-grade finishes** that hold up against moisture
- **High-quality hardware** that won't corrode from coastal air

Choosing the right materials now means you won't be redoing it in two years.

---

### ✅ Think About Flow and Functionality

The most beautiful kitchens and bathrooms are also the most functional. Before committing to any design, think through:

- How you (or your guests) actually move through and use the space
- Where storage is needed and what needs to be within easy reach
- Whether the current layout serves you or fights against you

Custom carpentry is the solution here—everything designed around your space, not forced into it.

---

### ✅ Plan Your Lighting

Lighting is one of the highest-impact, lowest-cost upgrades you can make. Think in layers:

- **Ambient lighting** for overall brightness
- **Task lighting** under cabinets and over work surfaces
- **Accent lighting** to highlight architectural details or custom woodwork

Warm LED lighting adds warmth and sophistication—perfect for the Puerto Vallarta aesthetic.

---

### ✅ Decide: Custom or Standard?

Off-the-shelf cabinets and fixtures save money upfront but often cost more in the long run—they don't fit perfectly, they look generic, and they don't hold up as well.

Custom carpentry offers:

- A precise fit for your unique space
- Higher-quality materials and craftsmanship
- A distinctive look that elevates the entire property

For rental properties especially, custom finishes signal quality and justify higher rates.

---

### ✅ Lock In Your Timeline

Work backwards from the start of high season (November) and build your project timeline:

- **May–June:** Finalize design and material selections
- **July–August:** Active construction and installation
- **September–October:** Finishing touches, staging, and professional photography
- **November:** Ready to welcome guests to a transformed property

Starting now gives you the full runway to do this right.
