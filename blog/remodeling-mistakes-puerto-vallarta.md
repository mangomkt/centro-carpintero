Renovating your home in Puerto Vallarta is exciting—but it can also become expensive fast if you don’t plan properly.

At Centro Carpintero PV, we’ve seen the same mistakes happen over and over again. The good news? They’re completely avoidable.

Here are the **top 5 remodeling mistakes** and how to avoid them.

---

## 1. Choosing the Wrong Materials for a Coastal Climate

Puerto Vallarta’s humidity, salt air, and heat can destroy low-quality materials quickly.

**Common mistake:**  
Choosing cheaper woods or finishes that warp, crack, or fade within months.

**Better approach:**  
Use durable materials like:
- Parota wood
- Marine-grade finishes
- High-quality hardware

These materials are built to last in tropical environments.

---

## 2. Poor Space Planning

A beautiful kitchen that doesn’t function well becomes frustrating every single day.

**Common mistake:**  
Ignoring workflow—placing appliances, cabinets, and storage without thinking about how you actually use the space.

**Better approach:**  
Design around:
- Movement flow
- Storage needs
- Daily habits

Custom carpentry ensures everything fits perfectly—not just visually, but functionally.

---

## 3. Ignoring Lighting Design

Lighting is one of the most overlooked elements in remodeling.

**Common mistake:**  
Relying only on overhead lighting.

**Better approach:**  
Layer your lighting:
- Ambient lighting (general)
- Task lighting (under cabinets, work areas)
- Accent lighting (design highlights)

Warm LED lighting works especially well in Puerto Vallarta homes, creating a welcoming and modern feel.

---

## 4. Not Having a Clear Plan Before Starting

This is where budgets spiral out of control.

**Common mistake:**  
Starting construction without finalized designs, materials, and timelines.

**Better approach:**  
Have a complete plan that includes:
- Detailed designs
- Material selection
- Project timeline
- Budget expectations

Planning upfront saves time, money, and stress.

---

## 5. Choosing Standard Instead of Custom

Off-the-shelf solutions rarely fit perfectly—especially in unique spaces like condos and custom homes.

**Common mistake:**  
Trying to force standard cabinets into non-standard spaces.

**Better approach:**  
Invest in custom solutions:
- Better use of space
- Higher quality craftsmanship
- A unique, tailored design

---

## Final Thoughts

A successful renovation isn’t just about how your home looks—it’s about how it performs over time.

Avoiding these mistakes can save you thousands and ensure your home stays beautiful for years to come.
